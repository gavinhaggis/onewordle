The shape system

Every puzzle has a shape

A shape is three numbers that add up to five. G letters are already in place. Y letters are shown but have to move. X letters aren't shown at all, so you have to come up with them. The grid counts every one-answer clue without a grey clash by its greens and yellows. Only the outlined cells go into the game, because playtesting found 1–2 greens and 1–2 yellows the most fun.
