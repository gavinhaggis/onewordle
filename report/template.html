Which shapes make the cut

Every unique, unambiguous one-row puzzle, grouped by how many greens and yellows its clue shows. Only the outlined cells (1–2 greens, 1–2 yellows) go into the one-row game. Rows with 4 greens are never used.

Difficulty

Five measures, one score

D = 0.30·U + 0.20·C + 0.25·X + 0.10·Y + 0.15·N

Each measure is turned into its percentile rank across the whole pool (0 = easiest puzzle, 1 = hardest), so they can be added together. The four bands are the quarters of D: gentle, medium, tricky, fiendish.
